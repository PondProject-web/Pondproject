Microscopy

Layer 2

Main Content

Microscopes were used to examine pond water and identify what kinds of microorganisms were present, including algae, bacteria, and other microscopic life. Samples from several ponds were compared to see how visible life differed by source.

Why Microscopy Matters

It gives the first visual look at the biodiversity inside the pond water.

Quick Points

  • Pond 1, 2, and 3 were observed.
  • Some days also included pond 0.
  • Bacteria were hard to track because they were tiny and moved fast.
