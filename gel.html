Agarose Gel Electrophoresis

Layer 6

Main Content

Agarose gel electrophoresis is a molecular biology technique used to separate and visualize DNA based on size and charge. DNA moves through an agarose matrix under an electric field, with smaller fragments moving faster.

Materials

Agarose gel, ionic buffer, electric current, and fluorescent dyes.

Applications

DNA analysis, cloning, PCR verification, and fragment separation.

Quick Points

  • DNA is negatively charged because of its phosphate backbone.
  • Fragments travel toward the positive electrode.
  • Smaller fragments move more easily through the gel pores.
