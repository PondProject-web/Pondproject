Gram Staining

Layer 4

Main Content

Gram staining was scheduled as part of the next phase of the experiment. This method uses multiple dyes to help distinguish bacteria based on cell wall structure, helping classify them as gram-positive or gram-negative.

Why It Helps

It provides a clearer way to identify bacteria after culturing them on plates.

Quick Points

  • Crystal violet, iodine, alcohol, and safranin are used in sequence.
  • The result helps separate bacterial groups visually.
