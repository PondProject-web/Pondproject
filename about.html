About the Project

Layer 1

Main Content

This project investigates microorganisms found in pond water. It combines visual observation, bacterial isolation, staining methods, and DNA analysis to better understand how bacteria grow and how different laboratory methods help identify them.

Project Goal

Study microbial life in pond samples and organize the results into a clear visual website.

Project Focus

Observation, culturing, identification, and genetic analysis.

Quick Points

  • Pond samples were examined from multiple locations.
  • The work focused mainly on bacteria instead of only algae.
  • The final site is designed to explain the full process clearly.
